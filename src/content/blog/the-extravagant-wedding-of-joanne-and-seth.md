The wedding of Joanne and Seth took place at the "Alamo Plactica Park Flower Garden". As guests arrived at the venue they were able to grab sunglasses because they did not want to be bonded by the couple's love. They also had bowling pins throughout the ceremony because they met in the bowling ally. The music for the night was by guitarist and vocalist, Chris Webb and he was amazing! When the ceremony started, the groomsmen and the family entered. Then the bridal party came out of the limousine and entered the garden. Then came the bride and her father! The flowers the bridesmaids and the bride had were from Brittany Tarkington from "Moss and Oak Floral". Afterwards everyone went to "Lala's" for the reception and had a great time!

Congratulations to Joanne and Seth. May your life be extraordinary!!
