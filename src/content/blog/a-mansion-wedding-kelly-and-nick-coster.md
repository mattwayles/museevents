On June 23rd, 2017 at Boettcher Mansion, Kelly and Nick were pronounced husband and wife. The ceremony began promptly at 5:00pm with the seating of the family followed by the procession of the bridal party and then it was time for Nick to see his bride-to-be. Kelly was escorted by her father, Marty while the song, “Can’t Help Falling in Love” was playing in the background. As soon as Kelly and Nick’s eyes met, you can see the tears in their eyes and you were able to see the love that they have for each other, it was perfect! Once the ceremony was over, the newlyweds were off to take photos by J La Plante Moira & Jesse, and their guests were enjoying cocktail hour on the front patio.

Once Kelly and Nick joined their wedding guests, dinner was ready to be served by Rocky Mountain Catering and everyone loved the food so much the guests came back for seconds! As everyone was settling down, the maid of honor and the best man gave their toasts and then it was time for dessert! The couple wanted something different so they had a s’mores bar. Once Kelly and Nick made each other s’mores, it was time for them to share their first dance together as husband and wife; they danced to the song, “Forever” by Bart Crow. As soon as they were finished sharing their dance together it was time for Kelly to dance with her dad and Nick to dance with his mom. They both chose touching songs for their dances and once the dances were over, the dance floor was open for the rest of the guests to join in on the celebration. They had danced the night away, making unforgettable memories!
