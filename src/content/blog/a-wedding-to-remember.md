What a beautiful night to be remembered by all who attended. Kyndra and Michael's wedding at Spruce Mountain Ranch was a sight to see. The simplicity of their decorations, ceremony, and reception created an ambiance that lit up the entire event. The aisle was lined with beautiful candles and rose petals, which made for a romantic atmosphere that every guest admired. The pink and white color theme made a light and lovely setting for both their ceremony and reception.

After the ceremony, every guest was welcomed into cocktail hour by adorable cake-pops, which were titled by the name of the guest and their table number. What a great idea to get guests to their tables for dinner, all while giving them a sweet treat.

Once to their tables, guests were touched by the incredible toasts by the wedding party. Dinner included salad, steak, fish, green beans, and mashed potatoes all precisely created by Catering by Design. Next, guests moved to the dessert table with all kinds of beautiful baked goods baked by the mother of the bride and the bride herself. Guests watched as the newlyweds cut their incredible wedding cake and then head out onto the dance floor for their first dance as man and wife.

After, the party started. Guests and the bridal party took to the dance floor and danced the night away. What an incredible evening that each and every guest enjoyed to the fullest. It will be remembered for years to come because of how amazing the night turned out to be.
