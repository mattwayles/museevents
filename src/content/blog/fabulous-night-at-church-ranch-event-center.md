Spring is officially in the air. Classy, glamorous and sophisticated have never been more appropriate words for a get together as they were for the event held at Church Ranch Event Center in Westminister. With my personal highlight being a raffle for a free bottle of Champagne towards the end of the evening.

The attendees were enthralled with a variety of performances including orchestra and soothing music by the DJ from A Music Plus.

Various caterers included Bread Winners, Greens Point, and Three Tomatoes. All of which presented great looking and delicious tasting food samplings.

For dessert, there were vodka and margarita cupcakes provided to us by Cupcake Libations, which I absolutely loved. Everyone in attendance was also in love with the Chocolate Fountain provided by A New Taste Sensation. And guests that night couldn’t get enough of the photo booth provided by All Digital studios.

Décor included colorful event designs and florals with modern touches that all seemed to compliment the start of the spring season. All and all The décor came together stunningly. Comfortable Couches were also on display by Couch Colorado. Silver Sparrow Photography provided us with their sample of amazing works on HD television. And not to mention, the wine was flowing and their Signature cocktails were a hit!

Good food and music, amazing company, and a warm inviting environment about sums up the night at Church Ranch Event Center. The Exotic Car Rental’s luxurious car was in display outside the venue. I thought this venue was perfect choice for any wedding reception or any event. It was more than an honor to be invited in the event Thanks Church Ranch Event Center from Muse Events.
