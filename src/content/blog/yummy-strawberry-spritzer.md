This photo made me wish for summer and all the outdoor weddings and parties it brings. This is a tasty recipe by Peak Beverage as thier Beverage of the Month. Here's the simplest recipe to happyness:

Shake a Strawberry Puree with

A Splash of Fresh Squeezed Orange Juice

and blend with Prosecco or Champagne

Garnish with mint and strawberry

Voila! Summer in a glass.

Enjoy!
