The ceremony was held at Saint Bernadette church on September 20th. The beautiful bride said I do in an amazing church venue, accented by orange and grey ribbons and assorted flowers. What made this wedding so memorable was the Marine Sword Send Off tradition which made this wedding so unique to the newlyweds. After the ceremony, the couple hosted a cocktail party at The Mount Vernon Country Club overlooking Look Out Mountain and the breath taking scenery around them. The couples simple yet inexpensive hanging name cards made it fun and easy for their guests to find their names and seating arrangements.

Awaiting them, was an elegant reception featuring amazing food and great spirits. The newlyweds served a fabulous steak as well as salmon which was cooked to perfection but the stories from the Best Man and Maid of Honor and the couple’s first dance was just as memorable. To end off their night the guests enjoyed their last cocktails of the evening and hit the dance floor leaving full and satisfied an with amazing memories. It was such an amazing wedding.
