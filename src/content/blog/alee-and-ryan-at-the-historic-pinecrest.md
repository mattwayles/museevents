On April 22, 2017, Alee and Ryan were pronounced husband and wife in front of their family and friends. Their ceremony took place at The Historic Pinecrest in Palmer Lake, Colorado. In the afternoon, Ryan was able to see his bride during their first look and she took his breath away! Soon enough they were going to be married. The ceremony started at 5pm that evening with Ryan walking down the aisle and then Alee walked down the aisle with her father. After an exchange of vows, rings, and a kiss they were pronounced husband and wife!

While the couple was off taking pictures by Aesthete Pro Photo, their guests were enjoying their cocktail hour with some delicious appetizers from the caterer of the night, Catering by Nikki. When the couple returned to their party, it was time to eat! There was a blessing from Alee’s Uncle Dan before they enjoyed their dinner. Once dinner was over, some toasts were made and then it was time to get the dancing started. To kick off the dancing was Alee and her father as they shared their father daughter dance and next was Ryan and his mother to share their mother son dance; Alee also shared a dance with her brother after, then the dance floor was open for all their guests to join and everyone was having a great time celebrating this wonderful couple!
