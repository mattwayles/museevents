This gorgeous video by J9 Video Productions captures the exquisite wedding ceremony in which Karen and Joe said I Do. The ceremony was held atop Vail Mountain on the Wedding Deck. The intimacy of the wedding included the bride's uncle as thier officiant and guests from China and the United States. After the ceremony, the couple hosted a cocktail party at the Arrabelle including gaming tables featuring craps, black jack, gai pow and roulette. That evening the brides parents invited close friends and family to a private reception at Terra Bistro. A wonderful day filled with love and excitement reflected the couple's love for each other and for family.
